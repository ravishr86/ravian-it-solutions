Frequently asked questions

Common questions about managed IT services.

What is a Managed Service Provider (MSP)?

An MSP manages and supports your business technology on an ongoing basis, including monitoring, maintenance, help desk support, cybersecurity and strategic planning.

Can you support our existing IT team?

Yes. We can act as your complete outsourced IT department or provide co-managed support alongside your internal team.

Do you support Microsoft 365 and Azure?

Yes. We provide implementation, migration, management and security services for Microsoft 365, Azure, Entra ID and Intune.

Do you only serve businesses in London?

We are based in London, Ontario and can support businesses locally and remotely depending on their requirements.
